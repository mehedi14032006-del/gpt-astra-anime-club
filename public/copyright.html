Copyright and Anti-Piracy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Our commitment

Kitsune+ respects the work of anime creators, studios and licensors. We only show video that is officially licensed, openly licensed or in the public domain, played through the rights holder's own channel or an embed they allow. When a title is not available to watch legally on our site, we show where it can be watched legitimately instead.

What is not allowed

  • Pirated or unauthorized streams, downloads or uploads
  • Illegal mirrors, scraped or ripped video sources, and torrent links
  • Bypassing DRM, paywalls or regional licensing
  • Proxying or re-streaming provider content without permission
  • Disguising or hiding any of the above

We do not use these methods, and we remove any link that is found to be unauthorized.

Where artwork and information come from

Posters, descriptions and scores come from catalog services (Jikan/MyAnimeList and AniList) and remain the property of their owners. We do not claim ownership.

Reporting infringement

If you own or represent the rights to a work and believe something on Kitsune+ infringes it, send a notice through the report page (choose “Copyright”). Please include: the work you own; the exact page or link on our site; a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized; a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for them; and your name and a way to reach you.

What happens next

We review notices promptly and remove or disable access to material that appears to infringe. If you believe content was removed by mistake, you may send a counter-notice through the same page explaining why.

Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly post or request infringing material will be closed.
