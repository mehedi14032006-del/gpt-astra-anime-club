Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

What we collect

When you create an account we store your email address, your plan (free or Premium), your role, and whether you confirmed you are 18 or older. If you use community features we store your ratings, reviews, favorites and watchlist. If you file a report we store what you submit, including an optional email address.

What we do not collect

We never see or store your card details; payments are handled by Stripe. We do not sell your personal information.

Services that handle data for us

  • Supabase stores accounts and site data and handles sign-in.
  • Stripe processes Premium payments.
  • Vercel hosts the site and may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested).
  • Jikan (MyAnimeList data) and AniList supply anime information and artwork.
  • YouTube and other official providers play videos when you press play on an embedded title. They may set cookies or collect data under their own policies.

Storage on your device

Your browser stores your sign-in session and may store preferences. It is not used to decide your plan or age status; those are checked on our servers.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your account and data by contacting us through the report page and choosing “Other”.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Mature titles require confirmation that you are 18 or older.

Changes

If we add services such as advertising, we will update this policy before using them.
