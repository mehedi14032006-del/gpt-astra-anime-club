Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

What we collect

If you create an account, we store your email address and your plan (free or Premium). If you confirm you are 18 or older, we store that confirmation so we do not ask again. We do not store your password in readable form, and we never see your card details.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and sign-in data.
  • Stripe handles Premium payments. Card details go directly to Stripe.
  • Jikan (MyAnimeList data) supplies anime titles, posters and trailers.
  • YouTube plays official trailers and free episodes. YouTube may set cookies when you play a video, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Advertising partners may show ads to free users. They may use cookies or similar technology to show ads and measure results. Premium members do not see ads.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads or at aboutads.info.

Children

Kitsune+ is not directed to children under 13, and mature titles require confirmation that you are 18 or older.

Your choices

Changes
